About Marcus Aurelius

Who was Marcus Aurelius?

Marcus Aurelius (121–180 AD) was Roman Emperor from 161 to 180 AD and one of the most important Stoic philosophers. He ruled during a period of military conflict and plague, yet found time to write his private philosophical journal, Meditations — a collection of reflections on duty, virtue, and how to live well under pressure. He is widely regarded as the last of the Five Good Emperors.

What is the book Meditations about?

Meditations is a series of personal writings by Marcus Aurelius, never intended for publication. Written in Greek while on military campaigns, it consists of twelve books of reflections on Stoic philosophy — covering how to deal with difficulty, control one’s thoughts, fulfil one’s duties, and face mortality with equanimity. It remains one of the most widely read works of ancient philosophy.

Was Marcus Aurelius a good emperor?

Marcus Aurelius is generally regarded as one of Rome’s greatest emperors. He spent much of his reign fighting wars on the empire’s frontiers, yet administered Rome with justice and restraint. He is praised for his modesty, his efforts to reform the legal system, and for governing according to his philosophical principles — though historians also note the contradiction of a Stoic emperor who persecuted Christians.

What are Marcus Aurelius’s most famous quotes?

Some of Marcus Aurelius’s most enduring quotes include “You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realise this, and you will find strength,” “The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way,” and “The soul becomes dyed with the colour of its thoughts.” All are drawn from his Meditations.

How did Marcus Aurelius die?

Marcus Aurelius died on March 17, 180 AD, near the town of Sirmium (modern-day Serbia), most likely from plague — possibly the Antonine Plague that had ravaged the Roman Empire for years. He died on campaign, still in service to Rome, consistent with the Stoic ideal he had written about all his life. He was 58 years old.
